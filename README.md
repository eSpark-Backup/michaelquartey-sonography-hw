To design a workable ultrasound probe that is cost effective and a comparable smaller in size.

The probe will have two sections:

**Section 1:**
- Will contain only the transducers on its PCB or minimum required peripherals.
- The number of transducers will depend on the designer's decision as to miniumum how many transducers will be required to produce a minimal entry-level ultrasound image.
- This section will connect to the section 2 through wires over a retractable wire connector interface.

**Section 2:**
- Will contain all the driving circuitry of transducers, its peripherals and the controllers involved in the processing of the transducer signals.
- The main purpose of this section is to provide the processing capability on this PCB and send the processed signals over WiFi to a mobile app for visual representation.
